1Overview

This privacy policy applies to the Noori: Urdu Kids Stories Android app ("Noori" or "the app"), developed by Faizan Darwesh ("we", "us").

Noori is a bilingual English and Urdu story app for children aged 3–8. We built it so that families can use it with complete peace of mind: the app does not collect, transmit, share or sell any information.

2Information we collect

None. Noori does not collect any personal or non-personal information. There are no accounts, logins, sign-ups, or forms, and we never ask for names, emails, photos, voice recordings, or location.

We have no servers that receive data from the app, so there is nothing for us to store, share, or sell.

3Data stored on the device

To remember your child's progress, the app saves a small amount of data locally on the device only:

DataWhyWhere
Stories completed & starsShow progress and badgesThis device only
Fireflies earnedThe in-app reward systemThis device only
Word Jar wordsVocabulary your child collectedThis device only
SettingsLanguage, sound and Bedtime modeThis device only

This data is never sent anywhere. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings.

4Internet, ads & permissions

  • The app does not request the internet permission and makes no network connections.
  • No advertising, no ad SDKs, and no in-app purchases.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • No access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, or files.
  • No social features, chat, or links that open without a parent gate.

5Children's privacy

Noori is designed for children and complies with the Google Play Families Policy. Because the app collects no personal information, no data about children is gathered, in line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the US, the GDPR and UK GDPR (including the Age Appropriate Design Code), and similar laws worldwide.

6Parent gate

Settings, the Parent Corner, and any link that leads outside the app are protected by a parent gate: a simple math question written in words (for example, "Seven plus five?"). This keeps young children from changing settings or leaving the app by accident.

7Your choices

Since we hold no data about you or your child, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete on request. You stay in full control: clear the app's data or uninstall it at any time to remove everything stored on the device.

8Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a future version of the app ever changes how data is handled, we will update this policy before that version is released.

9Contact us

Questions about this policy or the app? We are happy to help.
